About Learnify

Empowering aspiring developers with simple, practical, and hands-on coding tutorials.

Who We Are

Learnify is a free online platform dedicated to helping beginners and intermediate learners master web development. We provide easy-to-follow tutorials, interactive code editors, and real-world projects to help you build skills efficiently.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning web development accessible and fun for everyone. We aim to:

  • Provide simple and practical tutorials for HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Encourage hands-on learning through interactive examples.
  • Help learners build real projects that they can showcase.
